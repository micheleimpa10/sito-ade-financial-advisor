<idea>
**Design Movement**: Swiss Minimalism meets Editorial Luxury

**Core Principles**:
1. Restrained elegance — every element earns its place
2. Strong typographic hierarchy using serif display + clean sans-serif body
3. Monochromatic navy/cream palette with gold accents for warmth
4. Asymmetric layouts that feel editorial and premium

**Color Philosophy**:
- Navy (#1a2744) as the primary dark — evokes trust, stability, depth
- Warm cream (#f8f5f0) as background — softer than pure white, feels luxurious
- Gold (#c9a84c) as accent — signals premium, Swiss precision
- Soft warm gray for secondary text

**Layout Paradigm**:
- Full-screen hero with polaroid-style photo overlay
- Asymmetric two-column sections alternating left/right
- Card-based services grid with hover reveals
- Sticky header that transitions from transparent to frosted glass

**Signature Elements**:
1. Thin gold horizontal rules as section dividers
2. Large serif numerals for statistics (300+, 2+, 3)
3. Polaroid-style photo frames with handwritten-style captions

**Interaction Philosophy**:
- Smooth scroll with section fade-ins
- Language switcher with instant content swap (no page reload)
- Hover states that reveal gold underlines on nav links

**Animation**:
- Entrance animations: fade-in + slide-up on scroll
- Hero image carousel with crossfade (5s interval)
- FAQ accordion with smooth height transitions
- Language switch: brief opacity fade

**Typography System**:
- Display: Playfair Display (serif) — for headings, hero title
- Body: DM Sans — clean, modern, highly readable
- Accent: DM Sans Italic — for quotes and testimonials

## Selected Approach

**Chosen: Swiss Minimalism meets Editorial Luxury**

The design uses Playfair Display for headings (elegant, trustworthy) and DM Sans for body text (clean, modern). The navy/cream/gold palette evokes Swiss banking precision with warmth. The asymmetric editorial layout feels premium without being cold.
